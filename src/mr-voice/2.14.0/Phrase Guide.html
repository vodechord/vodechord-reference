02

Speak vs sing

Toggle with ◌ next to SAY IT. Both modes use the same markup; the base contour underneath differs. It's fixed for now; shape it with markup and F0 edits.

SPEAK

  • Arc: pitch falls in a straight line from +1 to −2.5 st across each breath group, then restarts after every rest.
  • Ends: ? . ! , shape the word they follow (table above).
  • Length: natural; only the last vowel before a rest or end gets 1.15×.
  • Glides: pitch follows the articulation speed.
  • Key: ignored unless you switch ♪+◌ on.

SING

  • Melody: an 8‑slot pattern, 0 +2 +4 +5 +4 +2 0 −3 st, one slot per vowel, repeating. Consonants take the next vowel's note.
  • Ends: ? . ! , are rests only, no pitch change.
  • Length: every non‑stop sound 1.7× longer.
  • Glides: portamento up to 90 ms between notes.
  • Key: applied when KEY is on.

Order of layers, bottom to top: base contour (arc or melody) → markup → your F0 edits → KEY snap → a held MIDI note (overrides all, glides back on release).

03

Word spaces and rests

A space is a join, not a pause. Use punctuation when you want silence.

JOINWHENSOUND
LinkMost spaces, and any space next to a consonant (p, t, s…)No gap. Breath and voice continue; the consonant does the separating.
DipVowel → vowel, e.g. "go up"40 ms breath dip (25 ms sung), voice stays on.
Tie +You want a slurAlways linked.
Break |You want words apart90 ms unvoiced gap.
Rest , ; . …Phrasing, cadence, musical spaceSilent. The voice releases, then shapes the next sound in silence; the next onset is softer. Drag the rest cell to set its length.

Timeline symbols: | short or medium rest · ‖ long rest · · word dip or break. A final voiced stop (/b d g/) gets a short breathy release ə.

04

Key constraint

SET IT

  • KEY on, choose a root and a scale in the timeline header.
  • LEARN: play the scale, or just the tonic chord, from any MIDI source. The first note is the root; it sets 1.5 s after the last note.
  • KEY ROOT is bindable in SETUP › BIND to change key mid‑song.
  • ♪ sing only · ♪+◌ speech too.

THE RULE

  • Each note moves to the nearest in‑key note.
  • A note exactly between two in‑key notes follows the melody: up if rising from the previous note, otherwise down.
  • F0 drags snap to in‑key notes; the lane shows in‑key lines, root brighter.
  • Held MIDI notes are never changed.
